# Customer Service, Sales & Office Administration — Portfolio Samples

A small portfolio of practical spreadsheet examples for organizing customer enquiries, comparing daily sales with payment references, and keeping routine office work visible. The examples are aligned with transferable customer-service, sales-support and administration skills.

## Projects

### Customer Support Ticket Tracker
A customer-enquiry log with priority and status dropdowns, follow-up dates, and a summary dashboard. Customer identifiers and case details are fictional placeholders.

### Daily Sales & M-Pesa Reconciliation
A basic sales and payment-reference workbook with calculated totals and checks for matched, missing, mismatched and unmatched entries. All Kenyan-shilling amounts and transaction references are invented for demonstration; they do not represent real payments.

### Office Administration Task & Asset Tracker
A simple task log and shared-asset register with status fields, due dates, and a summary dashboard. All sample tasks and assets are fictional.

## Open and use

Download a workbook and open it in Microsoft Excel or import it into Google Sheets. The sample formulas recalculate when the workbook is opened. Replace the sample rows before adapting a file to a real workflow, and do not put customer, staff, account, or payment details in a public copy.

## Accuracy and privacy

These are self-directed demonstration templates, not employer records, customer records, or evidence of results achieved in a particular job. Every sample name/code, task, amount, and transaction reference is fictional. The files are intended to show how common service, sales-support, and office-administration workflows can be organized; they do not claim production software or financial expertise.
