# Customer Service, Sales & Office Administration — Portfolio Samples

A small portfolio of practical spreadsheet examples for organizing customer enquiries, comparing daily sales with payment references, and keeping routine office work visible. The examples focus on customer-service, sales-support and administration workflows.

## Projects

### Customer Support Ticket Tracker
A demo customer-enquiry log with priority and status dropdowns, follow-up dates, and a summary dashboard. Sample entries show how to organize enquiries and next steps.

### Daily Sales & M-Pesa Reconciliation
A sales and payment-reference workbook with calculated totals and checks for matched, missing, mismatched and unmatched entries. Sample Kenyan-shilling amounts and payment references exercise the workbook's reconciliation checks.

### Office Administration Task & Asset Tracker
A task log and shared-asset register with status fields, due dates, and a summary dashboard. Sample tasks and assets show how to track routine office workflows.

## Open and use

Download a workbook and open it in Microsoft Excel or import it into Google Sheets. The formulas recalculate when the workbook is opened. Replace the sample rows before adapting a file to your workflow, and omit sensitive details from public copies.

## About this portfolio

These portfolio demos organize common customer-service, sales-support, and office-administration workflows into practical spreadsheet templates. The workbooks highlight enquiry tracking, reconciliation checks, task coordination, and asset review.
